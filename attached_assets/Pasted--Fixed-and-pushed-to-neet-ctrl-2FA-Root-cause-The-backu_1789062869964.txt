Root cause
The backup was not actually incomplete. The diagnostic CountingOutputStream was counting each byte twice because:

write(byte[], …) incremented the counter
Android’s FilterOutputStream also routed through write(int)
That produced exactly the error shown:

Expected: 58,209,632
Actual: 29,104,819
Fix
The counter now writes directly to the wrapped stream and increments only once.
