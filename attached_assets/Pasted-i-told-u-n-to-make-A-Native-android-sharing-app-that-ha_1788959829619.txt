Important speed limitation
I cannot truthfully promise “100% exactly like Quick Share” yet. Quick Share uses Android system-level Wi‑Fi Direct and hotspot fallback, while the current VoltShare implementation uses direct TCP over the same Wi‑Fi network.

That means:

On a strong 5 GHz or Wi‑Fi 6 network, transfer can be very fast.
Speed depends on both phones, Wi‑Fi quality, distance, and Android restrictions.
If the phones are not on the same local network, the current version will not discover them.
No software can guarantee 100% transfer speed or availability across every Android device.
For true Quick Share-style behavior, the next networking upgrade should add:

Wi‑Fi Direct discovery
Temporary device-to-device hotspot fallback
Automatic transport selection
Transfer resume after interruption
Multiple-file transfer queue
Background transfer notification
Pause and resume controls
The current implementation is now safe and organized, but I am deliberately not claiming it is already a full replacement for Quick Share until those Wi‑Fi Direct and resume features are added.
