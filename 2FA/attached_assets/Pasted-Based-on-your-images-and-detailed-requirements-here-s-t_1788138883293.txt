✅ Feature Breakdown (Full Implementation)

1. Password Type (Top toggle group)

· Random – fully randomized characters
· Memorable – pronounceable syllables
· PIN – numeric only (4–8 digits)
· Passphrase – word-based with separators

---

2. Length Control

· Slider with min 4 / max 64
· − / + buttons for fine adjustment
· Live numeric display next to slider

---

3. Character Options

· Checkboxes for:
  · Uppercase (A–Z)
  · Lowercase (a–z)
  · Numbers (0–9)
  · Symbols (!@#$%^&*)
  · Extended Symbols (optional: ~ | \ [ ] { } etc.)

---

4. Exclusions

· Ambiguous characters – auto-block: O 0 I l 1
· Similar characters – block S/5, B/8, G/6
· Custom exclusion box – type any chars to exclude

---

5. Password Rules (Advanced)

· Minimum count per type:
  · Uppercase, Lowercase, Numbers, Symbols
· Avoid repeated characters (aa, 111)
· Avoid sequential characters (abcd, 1234)

---

6. Passphrase Options

· Only visible when Memorable or Passphrase is selected
· Number of words (2–10) with − / +
· Separator dropdown:
  · Hyphen (-)
  · Underscore (_)
  · Period (.)
  · Space ( )
· Capitalize words (toggle)
· Include numbers (toggle)
· Include symbols (toggle)

---

7. Strength & Entropy

· Real-time strength bar with 5 levels:
  · Weak (red)
  · Fair (orange)
  · Good (yellow)
  · Strong (green)
  · Very Strong (bright green)
· Entropy estimate in bits (e.g., ~94 bits)

---

8. Live Preview

· Large, monospace font
· 👁 Show/Hide toggle
· 📋 Copy to clipboard with feedback
· 🔄 Regenerate without closing dialog

---

9. Generate Button

· Prominent, full-width or centered
· Triggers regeneration with current settings

---

10. Action Buttons

· Use Password – fills into active field
· Save to Credentials – saves securely (if integrated)
· Copy & Close – copies and dismisses dialog

🧠 Interaction Logic

· Changing any setting auto-regenerates the password (optional toggle)
· Exclusions and Rules apply immediately to preview
· Passphrase section auto-hides when not relevant
· Copy shows a toast/snackbar: "Copied!"
· Save opens a sub-dialog or inline field to name the credential
