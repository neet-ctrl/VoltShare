· Slider with min 4 / max 64
· − / + buttons for fine adjustment
· Live numeric display next to slider

---

3. Character Options

· Checkboxes for:
  · Uppercase (A–Z)
  · Lowercase (a–z)
  · Numbers (0–9)
  · Symbols (!@#$%^&*)
  · Extended Symbols (optional: ~ | \ [ ] { } etc.)

---

4. Exclusions

· Ambiguous characters – auto-block: O 0 I l 1
· Similar characters – block S/5, B/8, G/6
· Custom exclusion box – type any chars to exclude

---

5. Password Rules (Advanced)

· Minimum count per type:
  · Uppercase, Lowercase, Numbers, Symbols

· Avoid sequential characters (abcd, 1234)

· Include symbols (toggle)
